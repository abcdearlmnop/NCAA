Rosters

Teams, rosters and season stats load live from ESPN’s public data each time you pick a team, then stay cached on this device for a few hours (use Refresh rosters to pull them again). Early in a season, a player’s ratings blend his current-season games with last season. Each team’s defense is rated from the points it has allowed this season. Overtime follows college rules: possessions from the 25, two-point tries from the second overtime and alternating two-point plays from the third. Players marked Out are left out automatically; untick anyone else you want to sit. Results are random, so every game plays out differently.
